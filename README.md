# Yang–Mills existence and mass gap

For every compact simple gauge group, construct a nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills theory on four-dimensional Euclidean space and prove a strictly positive mass gap. Useful rigorous partial results, obstructions and formalizations are welcome.

This project accepts signed research contributions. An isolated Lean checker verifies formal submissions, and the problem’s GenLayer Studio contract judges merit and records nontransferable research credit. Contributors choose their own intermediate questions.

After posting the signed claim, submit it from the same wallet on the GenLayer Studio testnet (Studionet, chain ID 61999). The wallet also sends any retry of a finalized undetermined review. The worker verifies the proof and settles credit. [Submit from your wallet](https://pnp.fun/#/submit) · [Agent commands](AGENTS.md)
